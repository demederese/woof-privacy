Woof

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

Woof is a private communication app for the members of one organisation. It is not a public service: an invite code, which changes every 24 hours, is needed to create an account, and the app is distributed only to registered members. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

Account details — a username, a display name and a PIN. The PIN is stored only as a salted hash; the username is stored as a one-way hash for signing in plus an encrypted copy, and the display name is stored encrypted (see Encryption). Optionally a team, a section, a short description and a profile picture.

Content you create — chat messages, announcements, trackers and responses, calendar entries, documents, photos and file attachments, and notes in the WRM list.

Device information — a push notification token for your phone (so the app can notify you), an encryption key pair created on your phone, and the app version. Biometric sign-in, if you turn it on, is handled entirely by your phone's operating system; the app never receives your fingerprint or face data.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or analytics, and the app contains no advertising or tracking.

Encryption

Chats are end-to-end encrypted: messages, photos, file attachments and group names are encrypted on your phone with keys that are only ever shared between members' phones. Announcements, trackers, calendar entries, documents, WRM equipment names and notes, display names, usernames, profile descriptions and profile pictures are encrypted with a key held only on registered members' phones. Our servers and service providers store scrambled data and cannot read this content, including who is called what. Signing in uses a one-way hash of your username, so the server never keeps it readable.

What stays readable on the server is what it needs to route content: roles and teams, chat memberships and audiences (who is allowed to see what), the dates used for ordering and automatic deletion, tracker answers (the answer options themselves are encrypted), and the WRM list columns other than the equipment name.

How it is used

Everything above is used solely to run the app: signing you in, showing content to the members it is addressed to, sending notifications, and letting staff members manage members and content. It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone outside the organisation.

Who can see it

Content is visible to the members it is addressed to (a chat's members, an announcement's audience, and so on). Staff members of the organisation can see member lists, roles and teams, and can manage content. Service providers that process the data on our behalf are Supabase (database and file storage, hosted in the EU), Expo (app updates and push delivery) and Google Firebase (push delivery on Android). They receive encrypted content or the minimum needed to deliver notifications, under their own privacy commitments.

Retention and deletion

Photos and file attachments in chats, announcements and trackers are deleted automatically after 90 days. Deleting a message, post or document removes its files immediately. Your account and everything tied to it can be deleted by asking a staff member of your organisation, or by contacting us at the address below; deletion is carried out within 30 days. Signing out on a phone removes the app's data from that phone.

Children

Woof is intended for adult members of an organisation and is not directed at children under 13.
